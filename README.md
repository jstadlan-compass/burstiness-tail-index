# burstiness-tail-index
Code for the manuscript “**Ratio of Quantiles Indicates Burstiness with Fewer False Negatives than the Conventional Burstiness Parameter**” by Joshua Z. Stadlan, Michelle Birkett, Jason H. Rife

Dependencies:
- Statistics and Machine Learning Toolbox 
- `plfit`, `plpva`, `bplfit`, `getPDF`, `bplcutfit`, `bstexpfit`, `blgnormfit`, `bexpnfit`, `blrtest` in `powerlaw`  toolboxes by Aaron Clauset and Yogesh Virkar (requires both):

  - continuous: https://aaronclauset.github.io/powerlaws/ , associated with A. Clauset, C.R. Shalizi, and M.E.J. Newman, "Power-law distributions in empirical data" SIAM Review 51(4), 661-703 (2009). doi:10.1137/070710111
  - binned: https://aaronclauset.github.io/powerlaws/bins/ , associated with Y. Virkar and A. Clauset, Power-law distributions in binned empirical data. Annals of Applied Statistics 8(1), 89 - 119 (2014).
    
- dataset folder from Sano & Takeuchi’s public dataset: https://infoshako.sk.tsukuba.ac.jp/~databank/db/DB2023-02.zip, with data description https://commons.sk.tsukuba.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2023/10/dbp_2023-02en.pdf , associated with Takeuchi, M., & Sano, Y. (2024). “Burstiness of human physical activities and their characterization,” J. Comput. Soc. Sc. (2024). https://doi.org/10.1007/s42001-024-00247-w
